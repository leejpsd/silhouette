Privacy

Silhouette, the web app at silhouette-hybrid.web.app and the iOS app "Silhouette: Benefits Check". Effective November 24, 2026.

Silhouette checks which public benefits a household in California may qualify for. It is a project built for the #BuildHybridAI 2026 hackathon. It is not a government service and does not give legal or tax advice.

What stays on your device

  • Your answers to the questions, the documents you add (a paystub or a Form W-2) and the values read from them are stored only on your device, encrypted.
  • Eligibility is decided by rules that run on your device.
  • Questions you type yourself are answered only by the model on your device and are never sent.
  • "Delete everything" in the app erases all of it.

What can leave your device, and to whom

  • A document's silhouette, when you press Send, when you run "Run all three steps" on the judges' page, or when the model on your device cannot read the document reliably. In a silhouette every number, date and unknown word is replaced by a placeholder such as N12; the values themselves never leave. It is read by Google's Gemini models through Firebase AI Logic.
  • A program's public information and a prepared question, when you ask for a general explanation of a program. Nothing about you is included.
  • Technical checks that carry no answers or documents: Firebase App Check (reCAPTCHA Enterprise on the web, Apple App Attest on iOS), which confirms that a request comes from the real app; Firebase Remote Config, which provides the app's settings; and a Firebase installation identifier used by those services.

The app lists every request that left your device under "Leaving your device", with exactly what it carried.

These requests are processed by Google as described in the Firebase privacy and security information and the Google Privacy Policy. As with any website, the services can see your network address.

What Silhouette does not do

  • No accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.
  • It is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions: open an issue on the project's public repository, github.com/leejpsd/silhouette.
